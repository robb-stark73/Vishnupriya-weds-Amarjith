What To Wear

Wedding Dress Code & Colour Themes ❤️

As we celebrate our special day, we'd love for everyone to be part of the festivities by dressing according to the colour themes planned for each occasion. We are keeping the celebrations rooted in the beauty of a traditional Kerala Hindu wedding, with colours that complement the joy and elegance of each event.

🌼 Haldi – A Celebration in Yellow

For our Haldi ceremony, we'd love everyone to dress in shades of yellow! From soft pastel yellows to vibrant sunshine and mustard tones, let's fill the occasion with warmth, happiness and all things bright and beautiful.

🖤 Sangeeth – An Evening of Bold Elegance

For the Sangeeth, we're going for a richer, more dramatic colour palette. Everyone is welcome to wear dark and deep shades, preferably black, or bright red with some sparkles. Think glamorous, festive and effortlessly elegant!

We'd request you to please avoid wearing dark blue for this occasion.

❤️ Wedding – A Traditional Kerala Style Wedding

For the wedding ceremony, we warmly invite our guests to embrace the timeless elegance of traditional Indian attire. We would be delighted to see women draped in sarees of rich dark green or deep maroon, and men attired in kurtas of matching hues or complementary tones of ivory, beige, and antique gold—an earthy, regal palette crafted to harmonize with our traditional Kerala Hindu celebration.

Most importantly, come dressed with love and ready to celebrate with us. We can't wait to make these memories together! ❤️

Your presence will make our celebration even more special.
